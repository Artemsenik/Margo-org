Interim state of the documentation

One route. Many different forms of responsibility.

The images show no uniform infrastructure. Some animals live in sheltered private areas, others at open feeding points, in bushes or unfinished buildings. It is precisely these differences that will later have to be reflected in the knowledge, rehoming and care systems.

The farm is already earmarked as a further care location. As soon as its situation, animal numbers, photos and context are available, it will be placed within the route. The existing stations can later also be deepened with videos, confirmed animal numbers and individual animal stories.
