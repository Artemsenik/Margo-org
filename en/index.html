How it began

How personal responsibility becomes a shared task.

Years ago Margo began caring for a few street cats around Artemida. A handful of animals became many. A fire destroyed the beach café she ran — the animals remained, and with them the daily responsibility.

Today she drives her rounds, arranges treatments and carries much of it herself. This is exactly where the initiative comes in: organising knowledge, disclosing costs, sharing tasks.

Who shares the load

Margo does not carry this alone

A registered partner association

Murka-Katzenhilfe e. V. (Bochum Local Court, VR 4939) handles donations and rehoming to Germany.

Two veterinary practices

Vets4life and OmniVET treat the animals at reduced rates.

Four pet food shops

Pet City, Pet Shop Manos, Pet Point and Dogit grant discounts on food.

A windsurfing school

Contributes financially and is home to some of the cats in care.
