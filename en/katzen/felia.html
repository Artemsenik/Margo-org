Her character

What Felia is like

What we describe here is based on observations from her everyday life – not on guarantees.

Decides for herself about closeness

Felia does not demand attention. When she wants closeness, she comes – and that is what makes the moment special.

More open with space

With a garden and freedom to roam she has become noticeably more open. People who give her space get much closer to her.

Peaceful with other cats

According to observations so far, she gets on well with other cats.

Experience with dogs

Felia knows life with dogs. Individual assessment during the rehoming process.

Her story

Found as a little one – grown into her own cat

Felia was found alone as a very young kitten and taken in.

She has a mind of her own: never the cat who constantly demands attention – and that is exactly what makes her special.

With space and patience she opens up more and more. Anyone who respects her way finds an attentive, calm companion.

What suits her

Felia’s ideal home

Not a list of conditions – a description of where Felia flourishes, according to our observations.

Secured outdoor access recommended

As we cannot reliably judge whether indoor keeping alone would be enough for her, we would prefer a home with secured outdoor access.

Respect for her independence

Felia needs people who appreciate her calm, independent nature.

Places to retreat

Places where she can be undisturbed.

Other cats possible

Conceivable according to observation; individual assessment.

Responsible for rehoming

Murka-Katzenhilfe e. V.

The Margo Animal Care Initiative introduces Felia and her story. Application, vetting and rehoming are handled by Murka-Katzenhilfe e. V.

Rehoming is into a loving indoor home or a home with secured outdoor access. In a suitably safe environment, outdoor access can also be made possible.

All questions about the process, the adoption fee, the home check and the handover are answered by the association.
