His character

What Ali is like

What we describe here is based on observations from his everyday life – not on guarantees.

He seeks closeness

Ali is decidedly people-oriented and likes being picked up. Physical closeness is not something he has to learn first.

Gentle in his manner

He approaches people calmly and carefully. Bustle or brashness are not his way.

He avoids conflict

According to observations so far, Ali is peaceful towards other cats and avoids conflict rather than seeking it.

Quiet is part of it

As much as he enjoys closeness – he equally needs places to retreat where he can be left in peace.

Wet food is his thing

He is not fussy about food: he is especially fond of wet food.

A view outdoors

A balcony or terrace would be lovely for him – provided the area is made cat-safe.

His story

From found kitten to quiet companion

Ali was found as a small, motherless kitten together with his siblings. Margo took the group in and raised them.

Early on a problem appeared that is not immediately visible: Ali’s eyelashes grew inwards and irritated his eyes. For him this meant persistent pain.

Ali was operated on. According to the information available to us, the treatment has healed well.

What has remained is his character: a cat who trusts people and seeks their closeness.

What suits him

His ideal home

Not a catalogue of requirements – rather a description of where Ali flourishes, according to our observations.

Calm and loving

A home without constant bustle, where someone has time for him and returns his affection.

Retreat possible

Places where he may be undisturbed when he feels like it.

Secured balcony welcome

A balcony or terrace would be lovely – made cat-safe.

Keeping decided after review

Whether indoor keeping or secured outdoor access: this is decided case by case and according to the association’s requirements.

Other cats? Case by case

Ali is peaceful towards other cats. Whether living together works is assessed individually during the rehoming process – it cannot be determined in advance.

People who stay

Ali has learned to trust people. He suits someone who will carry that trust for the long term.

Medical status

Health and medical history

We deliberately keep this information separate from his story – it should be soberly verifiable.

What is confirmed

  • Ali is neutered.
  • Ali is vaccinated.
  • He had an eye problem: his eyelashes grew inwards and irritated his eyes.
  • This problem was treated surgically.
  • According to current information, his eyes have healed well.

What is still outstanding

  • An FIV and FeLV test is carried out before departure.
  • The final medical check takes place as part of the rehoming process.

We deliberately make no statement about findings that are not yet available. All medical information reflects the status known to us at the time of the last update.

Responsible for rehoming

Murka-Katzenhilfe e. V.

The Margo Animal Care Initiative introduces Ali and his story. Application, vetting and rehoming are handled by Murka-Katzenhilfe e. V.

This means: all questions about the process, the adoption fee, the home check and the handover are answered by the association. An application is not a commitment but the start of a careful review.
