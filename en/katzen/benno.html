His character

What Benno is like

What we describe here is based on observations from his everyday life – not on guarantees.

He seeks the company of people

Benno is decidedly people-oriented. Physical closeness and calm company are no side matter for him.

Peaceful with other cats

According to observations so far, Benno gets on well with other cats and does not look for conflict.

Experience with dogs

Benno has already had contact with dogs. The final assessment for a shared household is made individually.

Curious and relaxed

He explores his surroundings calmly and without haste – a cat who settles where he feels safe.

Enjoying open space

A balcony or terrace would suit him well – provided it is made cat-safe. Small glimpses outdoors enrich his day.

Indoor keeping possible

Benno is suited to indoor keeping. In a suitably safe environment, secured outdoor access is also conceivable – the association decides.

His story

Found, taken in, flourished

Benno was found as a small kitten – alone, in a situation that was not good for him. Fortunately he was discovered before anything worse could happen.

Tony took him in. What Benno had been through until then we do not know. But from the day he was taken in he had water, food, shelter and people who looked out for him.

The frightened found kitten has become a calm, friendly cat – one you can see has arrived.

Responsible for rehoming

Murka-Katzenhilfe e. V.

The Margo Animal Care Initiative introduces Benno and his story. Application, vetting and rehoming are handled by Murka-Katzenhilfe e. V.

Rehoming is into a loving indoor home or a home with secured outdoor access. In a suitably safe environment, outdoor access can also be made possible.

All questions about the process, the adoption fee, the home check and the handover are answered by the association. An application is not a commitment but the start of a careful review.
