Partners & supporters

Margo does not carry this alone.

Behind the daily care of an estimated 250–300 animals (as of 2026) stands a small, reliable circle of partners – financially, medically and in everyday life. They are what makes work on this scale possible at all.

Who helps carry the work

Three partners secure the essentials: money for food and treatment, a home for some of the cats, and food at fair terms.

Financial support · Germany

Murka animal welfare association

The German-based animal welfare association Murka supports Margo financially – specifically for food and veterinary visits. This funding covers a substantial part of the ongoing care.

Website Instagram
Partner business · home to ~25 cats

The windsurfing school

The windsurfing school belongs to Tony Frey, Margo’s sister. She supports the work financially and the school is at the same time home to around 25 cats who live and are cared for on the grounds. Margo herself works there during the day as a surf instructor – work and care merge into one another here.

Food at fair terms

Four pet food shops

Four pet food shops support Margo with discounts on food. The owners meet her work with a great deal of empathy – a noticeable difference given the quantities needed every day.

Medical care

Veterinary partners

Margo works with two experienced veterinary practices. Both are highly competent and treat the animals in her care at somewhat reduced prices.

Vets4life

Veterinary practice

Competent veterinary care for the animals in Margo’s charge – at reduced terms for the initiative. Also carries out neutering at the agreed rate.
