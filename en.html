Margo Animal Care Initiative

One woman. Up to 300 cats. A system being built.

In Artemida near Athens, Greece, Margo has been caring for street animals for many years. What began with a handful of cats has grown to approximately 250–300 animals, spread across several private care locations, a farm and multiple feeding colonies. She feeds them daily, arranges veterinary treatment and pays for much of it herself.

The Margo Animal Care Initiative turns this one-person effort into a structure that can be sustained: documented medical care, transparent finances, and responsible rehoming through an established German partner organisation.

Rehoming standards

  • Full health check, vaccinations, deworming, FIV/FeLV testing before departure
  • EU pet passport, microchip, valid rabies vaccination, TRACES registration
  • Honest character description including difficulties and open questions
  • Home check and application review by the partner association
  • No spontaneous or holiday rehoming
  • Post-adoption support

Focus remains on care and neutering in Greece — not on moving large numbers of animals abroad.
